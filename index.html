Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AgroDetect AI

What is AgroDetect AI?

AgroDetect AI is an AI-powered plant disease detection system that helps farmers identify crop diseases by simply taking a photo of the affected leaf.

Is it free to use?

Yes! AgroDetect AI is completely free for all farmers. We believe in empowering agriculture with technology.

Do I need internet?

Yes, you need internet connection to upload images and get analysis results. We're working on an offline version.

How do I scan a leaf?

Click on "Scan Now", take a clear photo of the affected leaf, and our AI will analyze it within seconds.

What kind of photos work best?

Clear, well-lit photos of the leaf against a plain background work best. Avoid blurry or dark images.

How accurate is the detection?

Our AI model has 95% accuracy in identifying common plant diseases across 50+ crop types.

Which languages are supported?

We support 10 languages: English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, and Punjabi.
